About Us

A little room for a good game.

Chessroom makes it easy to take a break, think a few moves ahead, and enjoy chess.

Chess, without the extra steps

Chessroom is a free browser chess game. There is no account to create: open the board, choose how you want to play, and make your first move.

♞

Your own challenge

Play against the computer at Easy, Medium, or Hard difficulty. Choose white or black and practice at your own pace.

♙

A shared board

Play with a friend on the same device. Flip the board between turns to give each player a comfortable view.

Built for a little pause

Hints, undo, move history, and sound controls help you settle into the game. There is no game clock, so you can take your time.

Pick up where you left off

Your position and preferences are saved in this browser when local storage is available. Come back on the same browser and device to resume. Clearing browser data or using another device will not carry that game over.
