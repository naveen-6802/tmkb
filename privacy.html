Privacy Policy

Your game. Your browser.

How Chessroom handles information in its current version.

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Playing without an account

The Chessroom game does not ask you to create an account, enter a name, or provide an email address to play. Computer matches run in your browser; playing with a friend uses the same device.

Information saved on your device

Chessroom uses browser local storage to remember your current position, move history, chosen mode, piece colour, difficulty, board orientation, and sound preference. This lets you resume a game in the same browser. The saved game is not synced to other devices.

You can remove this information through your browser’s site-data settings. Clearing site data also removes your saved game and preferences. Private browsing or blocked storage may prevent saving.

Cookies, analytics, and advertising

The current game code does not include advertising, analytics trackers, or its own cookies. Fonts and visual assets are served with the website. If advertising or analytics are introduced, this policy will be updated to describe those services and any applicable choices.

Hosting and access

The service that hosts this website may process ordinary connection information, such as IP address, browser details, and requests, to deliver and secure the site. If access to the site requires a hosting-provider sign-in, that sign-in is handled by the provider, separately from the game’s browser storage.

Contacting us

If you contact us, the information you choose to send may be used to respond to your question or investigate a reported problem. Do not send sensitive information. See our Contact Us page for available contact details.

Children’s information

The game does not request personal information from children. If you believe personal information about a child has been shared with the site operator, use the Contact Us page to raise the concern.

Updates and questions

Changes to this policy will appear here with a revised date. For questions about privacy or information you have sent directly to us, visit Contact Us.
