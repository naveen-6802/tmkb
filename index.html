Free online chess, at your own pace

Chessroom is a browser-based chess game for moments when you want to concentrate, challenge yourself or spend time with someone. A solo match against the computer offers a different challenge at each difficulty level, while local two-player chess makes it easy to share a game without separate accounts.

Whether you prefer a quick match during a break or a longer game in the evening, you can play on a desktop, tablet or phone. Your saved position lets you continue later rather than having to finish in one sitting.
